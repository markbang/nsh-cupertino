# nsh-cupertino 0.2.0

[简体中文](v0.2.0.zh-CN.md)

The rest of the Cupertino component set: the overlays, pickers and controls iOS
uses beyond settings — and the separators of 0.1.0 fixed at their root.

## Install

```toml
[dependencies]
nsh-cupertino = { git = "https://github.com/markbang/nsh-cupertino", tag = "v0.2.0" }
```

```json
{
  "rust": ["../nsh-cupertino", "."],
  "theme": "../nsh-cupertino/theme/theme.json"
}
```

## Added

- **`Sheet`** with medium and large detents, a grabber, a drag between them with
  the release velocity, a backdrop that dims only at large, and close by
  backdrop, Escape, back or a drag past medium.
- **`ActionSheet`** — actions in one 10 pt group, a cancel button in another,
  red destructive rows, blurred surfaces and the safe area.
- **`AlertDialog`** — iOS's 270 pt alert, two buttons side by side or three
  stacked, and the standard text-entry variant.
- **`ContextMenu`** — a long press or the secondary button, a lifted preview over
  a dimmed page, icons, hairlines and Escape.
- **`Picker`** — a snapping wheel with items that fade and scale with the
  scroll, a selection band, haptics and press-to-centre.
- **`DatePicker`** — an inline six-week calendar in the locale's language and
  week start, a compact popover trigger and a month/day/year wheel.
- **`SegmentedControl`** — two to five segments on a 32 pt track in a 50 pt
  target, with a sliding white thumb.
- **`Slider`** — a 28 pt thumb on a tinted rail, tick marks, keyboard and screen
  reader support.
- **`TabBar`** — 49 pt, 25 pt icons, 10 pt labels, count and dot badges, and a
  material that blurs the page under it.
- **`SwipeRow`** — leading and trailing actions behind a list row, snap open,
  and a full swipe that fires and springs back for an undo affordance.
- **`RefreshView`** and the same pull in `NavPage` — an indicator that drops and
  stretches with the drag, and pins while a refresh runs.
- **Materials** for `NavPage`, `NavigationBar` and `TabBar`: the `regular`
  material's translucent fill over a 25 pt backdrop blur, floating over the
  content. Where a platform draws no backdrop blur (Linux, GPUI) the fill alone
  carries the bar.
- 12 demo pages and 24 more screenshots, including the overlays open in light
  and dark.

## Fixed

- **Grouped separators.** Every row but the last of a group now draws its
  hairline at every display scale: the separator is one **device pixel**
  (`round(max(0.33 × DPR, 1)) / DPR`), where 0.33 pt alone rounded away at 1x
  and left whole sections without lines. The rule holds for plain, subtitle,
  destructive, disabled, leading-icon, chevron and checkmark rows; a layout test
  checks the rects at 1x, 2x and 3x and a screenshot test checks the pixels in
  light and dark.

## Not ported faithfully

- **Picker perspective.** iOS squeezes the wheel in 3D; NSH binds scale and
  opacity, not a rotateX per row, so items fade and shrink but do not turn.
- **Context menu on phones.** `nsh/base` presents a context menu as a bottom
  sheet on touch screens rather than an anchored menu beside the preview; the
  menu is anchored on desktops and the web, which is also where the preview sits
  behind it.
- **Sheet scroll handoff.** A drag moves the sheet from its grabber and header,
  not from the content at its top, as iOS's larger sheets do.
- **Today's date** is read from the engine's wall clock in UTC; a user far from
  UTC may see tomorrow's or yesterday's number highlighted near midnight.
- **Swipe to delete** reports the action; removing the row and showing an undo
  bar are the app's (the demo shows both hooks).
- **Segmented control size.** The visible track is 32 pt (iOS's), inside the
  50 pt target the brief asked for.
